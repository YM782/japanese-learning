## How to evaluate a resource（如何判断资料质量）

1. Prefer the author's original repository or official site（优先使用作者原仓库或官方网站）.
2. Check the latest commit, release, and issue activity（查看最近提交、版本和问题区）.
3. Check whether explanations and examples are accurate（核对语法解释和例句）.
4. Keep a note of the date when the source was checked（记录核对日期）.
5. Do not treat community notes as an official JLPT syllabus（社区笔记不等同于官方考试大纲）.
